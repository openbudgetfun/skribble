# Give combo fields consistent spacing and a legible indicator

Separate the field border from menu rows, inset and center the selected label, and replace the cramped hatched arrow with an outlined triangle. Field and menu height grow with text scaling. Long selections keep space for the indicator, right-to-left layouts place it on the trailing edge, and empty fields retain their border. Wrapped menu items preserve their enabled state and callbacks.
