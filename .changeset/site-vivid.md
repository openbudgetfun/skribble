# skribble.dev is brighter and moves

The landing page uses the new sunny colours: sticker-chip section labels, a highlighter-yellow marquee and closing, and colour-coded cards. The hero copy springs in line by line, hand-drawn confetti boils around the headline and drifts with the pointer, and cards lift on hover. Everything stays still when your device asks for reduced motion.
